Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 1, 2026

InvoicePriceTracker stores invoice, supplier, product, price history, and account data for the company account that you log into.

Data We Process

  • Account information: name, email, company name, role, and login status.
  • Invoice data: supplier, invoice number, date, totals, item rows, discounts, gift/free item calculations, and review status.
  • Recognition data: OCR text, AI recognition JSON, templates, aliases, corrections, and confidence/warning metadata.
  • Local image data: invoice images are stored locally by default. Cloud sync does not upload original images unless a future migration feature is explicitly used by the user.

Cloud Sync

Cloud sync stores structured invoice data and product data in the backend database by company. Each request is authenticated and scoped to the user's company.

AI Processing

When AI Vision recognition is used, invoice images may be sent to the backend and processed by the configured AI provider for recognition. API keys are stored only on the backend.
